# Authority UX

## Goal

Users must always know:
- who can act
- what action means
- whether an action is recommendation, review, approval, publication, activation, or control

## Action taxonomy

Distinguish:
- read
- recommend
- draft
- simulate
- review
- approve
- reject
- publish
- activate
- transition
- control
- retire/delete
- external effect

## Rules

- recommendation must not look like approval
- generation must not look like activation
- draft save must not look like publication
- Board/Owner actions receive stronger confirmation
- destructive/irreversible actions require appropriate protection

## Spatial rule

3D objects may navigate to authority context.

High-stakes authority actions remain in structured governed controls.
