# Character Proportions

## Goal

Readable miniature professionals with adult identity.

## Direction

- slightly enlarged head
- compact torso
- shortened but adult-proportioned limbs
- expressive hands
- readable shoes
- stable center of gravity
- clean silhouette at small scene scale

## Avoid

- infant proportions
- giant head / tiny body caricature
- realistic anatomical detail that disappears at scene scale
- identical body shape for every role

## Variation

Allow controlled variation in:
- height
- shoulder width
- stance
- head shape
- limb proportion
- silhouette

without turning role identity into stereotypes.

Exact ratios remain open until side-by-side prototype evaluation at:
- close inspector scale
- standard office scale
- distant HQ overview
