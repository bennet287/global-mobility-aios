# Character Interactions

## Environment interaction

Characters may interact with:
- desk/work surface
- Mission Table
- Evidence Wall
- system display
- Board/authority area
- coffee/lounge
- handoff object

## Character-character interaction

### Ambient
Casual non-semantic gestures.

### Semantic
Supported:
- conversation
- handoff
- Mission collaboration

Semantic interactions require contract backing.

## Selection

Selected employee:
- receives subtle focus
- may orient toward camera/context
- does not stop canonical state
- exposes structured Employee context

## Gaze

Gaze can support:
- object focus
- conversation
- selected state

Do not overuse eye contact with user; keep behavior professional and natural.
