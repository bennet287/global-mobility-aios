# Character Personality Language

## Principle

Personality is expressed through subtle presentation behavior, not fictional biography.

## Presentation dimensions

- movement tempo
- gesture amplitude
- posture
- gaze frequency
- idle rhythm
- interaction with objects
- spatial comfort distance

## Example archetypes

### Deliberate
Slow, measured, low gesture amplitude.

### Analytical
Frequent small gaze shifts, device/surface interaction.

### Vigilant
Controlled stance, alert head/gaze behavior.

### Methodical
Steady reading/comparison gestures.

### Dynamic
Higher movement frequency, quicker transitions.

## Boundary

Personality may not imply:
- emotion
- intent
- confidence
- availability
- workload

as canonical fact unless supported.

It is presentation character, not a hidden psychological model.
