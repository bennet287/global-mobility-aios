# Specialist Role Direction

## Regulatory Intelligence
Evidence/source motifs, comparison gestures, research-table interaction.

## Operations
Practical styling, higher locomotion frequency, case/Mission movement.

## Security / SOC
Focused posture, disciplined gestures, technical/security surfaces.

## Engineering
System/prototype interaction, analytical motion, modern technical wardrobe.

## Product
Collaborative posture, Mission/product boards, balanced technical/business cues.

## Document / Eligibility / Visa roles
Evidence/document interaction, precise review gestures, clear professional identity.

## HR / Recruitment
People/organization interaction cues without generic “friendly HR” stereotypes.

## Communications / Marketing
Presentation/storytelling object language without becoming theatrical.

## Finance
Analytical evidence/numeric surface interaction.

All specialist archetypes must map to actual AIOS role definitions.
