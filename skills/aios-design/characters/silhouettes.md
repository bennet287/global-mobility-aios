# Character Silhouette System

## Principle

Role identity should be partially readable before labels.

## Silhouette variables

- shoulder line
- outerwear shape
- trouser/skirt geometry where used
- footwear
- hair mass
- prop silhouette
- stance
- posture
- movement rhythm

## Department cues

Executive:
clean, spacious silhouette.

Technology:
architectural/modular layers.

Security:
sharper geometry.

Regulatory:
precise/research-oriented.

Operations:
practical, mobile.

## Seniority cues

Use:
- posture
- garment structure
- movement economy
- prop language

rather than simply darker suits or larger bodies.

## Acceptance

At default office camera, silhouettes should remain distinguishable without creating costume-like exaggeration.
