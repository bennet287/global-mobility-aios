# Communications V2

## Principle

Communication is contextual organizational/case interaction, not a generic global chatbot.

## Context types

- Employee conversation
- Mission conversation
- Case communication
- Evidence discussion
- Board/authority discussion
- automated communication

## Draft lifecycle

Clearly distinguish:
- generated draft
- edited draft
- reviewed
- approved where applicable
- scheduled
- sent

## AI identity

If an AI employee is participating, show the employee identity and context.

Do not imply human authorship where it is AI-generated.

## Authority

Messaging/proposal UI does not bypass approval or external-action boundaries.
