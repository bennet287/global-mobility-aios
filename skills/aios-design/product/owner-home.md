# Owner Home / Cockpit V2

## Role

Owner Home is an executive situation room.

It is not a generic KPI dashboard.

## First viewport questions

1. What is happening?
2. What needs me?
3. What is the organization doing?

## Composition

Recommended zones:

### Situation
Short operational summary based on supported state.

### Needs Attention
Board/Owner authority, critical blockers, material evidence issues.

### Live Organization Preview
Miniature HQ view or structured fallback.

### Missions
Active/blocked/recently changed Missions.

### Recent Meaningful Change
Narrative/temporal activity rather than raw event firehose.

## Information depth

Primary:
- meaning
- action
- context

Secondary:
- evidence
- provenance
- technical contract details

## Counts

Never show a count/presence claim without supported backing state.

## Owner action

Owner actions use designated authority/intervention primitives.

They must not be hidden inside generic card buttons.

## Responsive

Desktop may show spatial preview + attention side-by-side.

Mobile prioritizes:
1. attention
2. Mission status
3. structured organization snapshot

rather than shrinking the full desktop scene.
