# Board Room V2 Product Specification

## Goal

Make high-stakes authority clear and calm.

## Primary view

Show:
- decision requiring authority
- recommendation/outcome context
- evidence strength
- material uncertainty
- affected Mission/work
- lineage
- authorized actions

## Interaction

Limit equal-weight actions.

Approval/rejection must be visually explicit and difficult to trigger accidentally.

## Provenance

Detailed decision/source lineage remains one level deeper.

## Spatial connection

Selecting the architectural Board Room opens this workspace.

The 3D room itself does not approve/reject.
