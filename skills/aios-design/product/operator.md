## Goal

Optimize for expert execution without creating a parallel executive dashboard.

## Work Home

Prioritize:
- cases/work needing attention
- blockers
- review queues
- next actions
- recent relevant change

## Dense workflows

Use structured:
- tables
- forms
- local navigation
- filters
- detail inspectors

Do not replace dense professional workflows with large decorative cards.

## Contextual tools

Authority appointments, submissions, agency assignments, checklists, validation, automation, coaching, and specialist domains should be reachable from relevant work context and Tools.
