# Benchmark Product Analysis Policy

When evaluating benchmark products, record:

1. interaction principle
2. why it works
3. what does not fit AIOS
4. AIOS-specific adaptation
5. accessibility/performance consequence

Do not copy skins.

Useful benchmark dimensions may include:
- executive information hierarchy
- command/search interaction
- spatial selection
- architectural visualization
- character readability
- timeline/replay
- evidence/provenance
- dense professional workspaces
- mobile simplification

The goal is to develop an original AIOS product language.
