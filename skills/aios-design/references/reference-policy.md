# Reference Policy

## Purpose

AIOS may use reference products, architecture, animation, character art, and user-supplied inspiration to understand:

- proportion
- polish
- composition
- interaction quality
- material language
- motion quality
- architectural mood
- readability

References are not licenses to copy.

## Character references

References may inform:
- compact miniature proportions
- expressive adult faces
- strong silhouettes
- premium animated-film polish

AIOS characters must remain original.

Do not reproduce:
- franchise-specific faces
- costumes
- logos
- signature character traits
- exact proportions intended to imitate a protected character identity.

## Product references

Extract principles, not skins.

Record:
- what works
- why it works
- what is unsuitable for AIOS
- how AIOS adapts the principle

## Architecture references

Use architecture references to study:
- circulation
- hierarchy
- atrium/terrace relationships
- light
- materials
- spatial memory
- miniature-model readability

The AIOS HQ must remain a coherent original world derived from organizational topology.
