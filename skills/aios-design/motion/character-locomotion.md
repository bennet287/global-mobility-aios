# Character Locomotion

## Principle

Locomotion is a presentation system, not a claim of physical real-world presence.

## Ambient local movement

May include:
- desk-to-coffee
- desk-to-window
- local repositioning
- lounge movement

Must remain clearly presentation-only.

## Semantic locomotion

May occur when a supported event/relationship justifies it:
- handoff
- Mission collaboration
- canonical conversation
- Board/authority event

## Pathing

Use:
- bounded navigation meshes/paths
- predictable room anchors
- collision avoidance
- short routes
- no chaotic crowd behavior

## Avoid

- random long-distance wandering
- repeated pacing
- motion that implies work transfer without canonical event
- users waiting for character travel before opening details

Structured navigation must remain immediate.
