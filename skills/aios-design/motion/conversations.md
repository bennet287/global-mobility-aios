# Conversation Motion

## Canonical conversation

When a supported conversation exists, participants may:
- orient toward each other
- gather at a collaboration point
- show a conversation marker
- open structured conversation context

## Transcript boundary

Do not fabricate speech, transcript content, or semantic claims.

## Ambient social gesture

Ambient gestures are distinct from canonical conversation.

They should not receive the same canonical conversation indicator.

## End state

When canonical conversation closes/changes lifecycle, participants return to supported work/ambient presentation.

## Reduced motion

Use static participant grouping/marker and structured conversation panel.
