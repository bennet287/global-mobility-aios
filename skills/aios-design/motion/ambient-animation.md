# Ambient Animation

## Purpose

Ambient animation gives the organization life without claiming organizational facts.

## Allowed examples

- breathing
- blinking
- glance shifts
- posture adjustments
- stretching
- local typing gestures
- reading tablet
- sipping coffee
- local walk
- looking through a window
- casual non-semantic social gesture

## Rules

Ambient animation is always presentation-only.

It cannot create:
- WorkItem execution
- conversation
- handoff
- blocker
- approval
- presence claim
- productivity claim

## Frequency

Ambient animation should be sparse and varied.

Avoid:
- synchronized loops
- constant fidgeting
- excessive movement
- animation that distracts from real semantic events

## Reduced motion

Reduce or remove ambient loops; preserve identity through static posture and state cues.
