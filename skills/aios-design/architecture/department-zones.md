# Department Zones

## Principle

Departments feel distinct without becoming themed attractions.

## Executive
- quiet
- high negative space
- refined materials
- fewer work objects
- strategic surfaces

## Technology
- precise
- modular
- visible systems/prototypes
- cool task-light accents

## Security
- controlled
- low clutter
- stronger spatial boundaries
- focused displays

## Regulatory / Evidence
- warmer
- research/library cues
- source/evidence surfaces
- comparison tables/walls

## Operations
- active
- open
- collaborative
- Mission/case stations
- higher circulation

## Rules

Department identity uses:
- architecture
- material
- furniture
- lighting
- object type
- spatial rhythm

not giant color-coded room walls.

Department accent color is secondary to truth/status.
