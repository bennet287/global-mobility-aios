# Mission Rooms

## Role

Mission Rooms spatialize active Mission collaboration.

They are backed by the Mission projection.

## Active state

May show:
- Mission identity
- supported participants
- related work objects
- subtle active illumination
- relevant evidence/decision objects where linked

## Blocked state

May show:
- blocker object
- restrained warning treatment
- participant attention only if supported

## Awaiting Owner

May show:
- authority connection
- Owner attention cue
- Board relation where applicable

## Rules

Do not:
- invent participants
- invent meetings
- invent conversations
- infer physical presence
- imply a Mission is “busy” from decorative animation

## Interaction

Selecting a Mission Room opens:
- Mission overview
- participants
- work
- blockers
- evidence
- decisions
- history
