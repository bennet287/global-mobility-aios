# Evidence Lab

## Role

The Evidence Lab spatializes evidence/source work.

## Spatial objects

May represent:
- VerifiedRule
- SourceSnapshot
- official source
- document
- professional review
- evidence bundle

## Visual language

Use:
- warmer research materials
- comparison surfaces
- source walls
- evidence tables
- archival precision

Avoid:
- literal paper-file clutter
- generic sci-fi holograms
- fake document text in the scene

## Interaction

Select an Evidence Object:
1. focus
2. compact source/verification summary
3. open Evidence workspace
4. inspect provenance/history

## Truth

Source authority, freshness, verification, memory/history must remain explicit.
