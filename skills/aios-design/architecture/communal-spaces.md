# Communal Spaces

## Purpose

Communal space gives the organization warmth and persistence.

Required:
- coffee bar
- lounge
- courtyard/greenery
- informal meeting niche
- terrace/soft seating

## Ambient behavior

Allowed:
- coffee
- casual local movement
- stretching
- looking outside
- idle social gestures

These are presentation-only.

## Semantic boundary

A casual ambient gathering is not a canonical conversation.

Canonical conversations must use separate visual semantics.

## Design

Communal spaces should feel premium and contemporary, not like a game social hub.
