# Architecture Materials & Lighting

## Material palette

Directional:
- warm stone
- refined wood
- smoked/clear glass
- brushed metal
- dark technical composite
- soft textiles
- controlled greenery

## Lighting

Use:
- soft ambient architectural light
- task lighting by department
- restrained accent light
- readable character lighting
- clear focal hierarchy

Avoid:
- nightclub neon
- excessive bloom
- dramatic darkness that hides employees
- different lighting styles per room with no system

## Time atmosphere

Morning/day/evening may affect environmental light.

It must not imply canonical employee work hours or presence.
