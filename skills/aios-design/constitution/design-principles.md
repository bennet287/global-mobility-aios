# AIOS V2 Design Constitution

## Constitutional principles

1. **Truth hierarchy before visual hierarchy.**
2. **What matters before how it is stored.**
3. **Complexity is progressively disclosed.**
4. **Not everything receives equal visual weight.**
5. **Authority-required events are unmistakable.**
6. **Recognition beats recall.**
7. **Spatial and temporal continuity are preserved.**
8. **Animation may express canonical state but may never manufacture it.**
9. **Employees must be recognizable as identities.**
10. **Architecture communicates organizational topology.**
11. **Primary actions provide immediate visible feedback.**
12. **Essential spatial information has a structured accessible equivalent.**
13. **Canonical, historical, memory, prediction, simulation, recommendation, and human authority are visually distinguishable.**
14. **Aesthetic quality is functional product quality.**
15. **No component exists merely because a design trend is fashionable.**
16. **The same domain object behaves consistently across views.**
17. **Focused surfaces expose only a small number of equal-weight primary actions.**
18. **Uncertainty and unsupported state remain visible.**

## Product personality

AIOS should feel:

- calm
- capable
- premium
- deliberate
- contemporary
- governed
- alive
- human-readable
- spatial when useful
- forensic when necessary

## Fashionability

Use durable contemporary design:

- architectural composition
- disciplined typography
- fine material hierarchy
- controlled depth
- polished motion
- distinctive characters
- meaningful spatial world

Avoid trend dependence:

- neon cyberpunk
- purple AI gradients
- excessive glass
- hologram spam
- card farms
- decorative motion

## Design balance

Structured UI is optimized for operation.

Living Organization is optimized for organizational understanding, identity, continuity, and presence.

The product must not become a game.
