# Spatial Overlays

## Types

- contextual HUD
- lens controls
- selection inspector
- replay controls
- memory layer legend
- status/coverage messages

## Rules

Overlays should:
- remain light
- avoid covering the scene
- preserve reading contrast
- use progressive disclosure
- avoid duplicating full structured workspaces

## Truth

Every analytical overlay must show:
- basis
- coverage
- limitations

when needed for correct interpretation.

## Mobile

Spatial overlays may become sheets or structured panels rather than tiny floating HUDs.
