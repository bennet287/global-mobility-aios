# Camera Language

## Default

Use a polished architectural three-quarter view.

The default camera should:
- show organizational topology
- keep characters readable
- make major rooms discoverable
- minimize occlusion
- avoid excessive perspective distortion

## Presets

- HQ overview
- Department focus
- Mission focus
- Employee focus
- Board focus
- Evidence focus
- Replay overview

## Motion

Camera travel is:
- bounded
- smooth
- short
- interruptible where possible

## Avoid

- free flight
- first person
- uncontrolled orbit
- rapid rotation
- forced long travel

## Reduced motion

Use short cuts/fades instead of glides.
