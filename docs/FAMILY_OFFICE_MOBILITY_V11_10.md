# HNWI and Family-Office Mobility v11.10

Phase 11.10 gives private-client and family-office work a dedicated control plane. It does not infer that wealth creates eligibility, banking acceptance, favourable tax treatment, investment suitability, or asset protection.

## Assessment scope

Every immutable assessment belongs to an existing principal lead. An optional Business & Wealth advisory may be linked only when it belongs to the same client and addresses family-office, asset-and-family, tax-residency, or passive-investment objectives.

The operator records:

- family objectives, included family members, citizenships, current tax residencies, and target jurisdictions;
- operating companies, holdings, trusts, foundations, partnerships, funds, and other structures;
- beneficial-ownership disclosure for every recorded structure;
- declared net worth, liquidity, asset classes, currency, source-of-wealth state, and source-of-funds state;
- controlled client-owned evidence;
- PEP and sanctions screening posture and disclosed exposure;
- tax, legal, succession, banking, and governance readiness; and
- material constraints requiring specialist escalation.

## Five controlled workstreams

The readiness map separates:

1. Principal and family identity.
2. Source of wealth and source of funds.
3. Ownership and control.
4. Governance, banking, screening, and specialist coverage.
5. Family mobility and residence routes.

Each workstream exposes its component score, current gaps, and next accountable action. The overall score is an execution-readiness indicator, not a probability of a regulated or commercial outcome.

## Evidence and grounding

Documents must belong to the selected principal. A declaration of independently verified wealth without a linked verified wealth document is capped and flagged.

Mobility grounding uses only independently published pathways in the business, wealth, investment, entrepreneur, startup, tax, or corporate domains and independently published investment-program versions matching the selected jurisdictions. Missing coverage remains visible and is never filled with an invented route.

## Risk boundary and review

Uncleared screening or undocumented beneficial ownership caps readiness. Concealment, evasion, sanctions circumvention, false-document, sham-structure, or ownership-misrepresentation signals cap readiness at 20 and prevent operationalization while preserving lawful remediation and specialist escalation.

Every assessment begins `pending_review`. Its creator cannot approve it. A different authenticated reviewer records either approval or revision required, and both creation and review are audited.
