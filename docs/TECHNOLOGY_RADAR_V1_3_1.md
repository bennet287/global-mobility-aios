## 6. LLMLingua-2 decision

LLMLingua-2 is the selected primary compression pilot behind an AIOS-owned port.

```text
Context Broker
      ↓
ContextCompressionPort
      ↓
LLMLingua2Adapter
```

This is not production adoption and not a declaration that semantic compression is inherently safe.

Permanent compression rules:

```text
compressed context = derived execution artifact
compressed context != source truth
```

For initial R3–R5 use, zero semantic compression is the default for:

- mandatory Evidence;
- critical VerifiedRules;
- exact money/dates/identifiers;
- authority/autonomy/risk constraints;
- policy constraints;
- contradictions;
- material-action parameters;
- source/version identifiers.

Pilot measurements:

- task quality vs uncompressed baseline;
- protected-span integrity;
- relevant-context retention;
- compression ratio;
- latency;
- token reduction;
- critical-value preservation;
- reproducibility by compressor/model/policy revision.

Advance only when measured savings occur without unacceptable semantic loss.

## 8. AI Economics / Cost Governor

Cost is optimized below a constitutional quality floor.

Target metric:

> **€ / successful governed outcome**

Future accounting may include:

- provider tokens;
- local GPU/compute;
- retries;
- independent verification;
- failed Missions;
- latency/SLA penalty;
- human intervention;
- cache/storage overhead.

Budget pressure may reroute, defer, restrict or escalate; it may not silently lower required quality.
