# Global Intelligence Evidence Filters v10.11

## Purpose

This Phase 10C increment lets operators narrow the live global intelligence dashboard
without creating a second interpretation layer. One selected evidence scope is applied
to headline metrics, change lists, the country activity heatmap, and the Opportunity
Radar.

The filters summarize evidence posture. They do not rank destinations, predict visa
outcomes, or turn pending changes into verified rules.

## Filter dimensions

`GET /api/v1/global-intelligence/dashboard` now accepts:

- `freshness`: `fresh`, `stale`, `never_checked`, `inactive`, or `unmonitored`;
- `coverage`: `ready`, `gap`, `not_required`, or `unregistered`;
- `authority_id`: an active regulatory-authority UUID;
- `confidence`: `high`, `medium`, `low`, or `unknown`;
- `materiality`: `informational`, `material`, or `critical`;
- `review_state`: `pending_review`, `approved`, `rejected`, or `published`.

Every filter defaults to `all`. Unsupported values fail with HTTP 400 rather than being
silently ignored.

## Evidence derivation

Freshness comes from the source monitor attached to the exact official source. A monitor
is fresh only when it is active and its most recent successful check is inside the larger
of two scheduled intervals or 24 hours.

Coverage comes from the active canonical jurisdiction-registry release:

- `ready` means every required authority, source, monitor, verified-rule, and
  immigration-rule-assessment gate passes;
- `gap` means the jurisdiction is coverage-required and at least one gate is missing;
- `not_required` is retained for special or unpopulated registry areas;
- `unregistered` means no active registry entry can be linked.

Authority comes from the official source's governed regulatory-authority relationship.

Confidence is never generated by the dashboard. It uses, in order:

1. the verified rule linked to the regulatory change;
2. an accepted governed classification proposal;
3. the latest governed classification proposal;
4. `unknown` when no governed confidence record exists.

Bands are deterministic: high is at least 0.90, medium is 0.70–0.899, and low is below
0.70.

## Consistent aggregation

The filtered change set drives:

- changes and countries updated today;
- change-type, review-state, and materiality counts;
- programme, processing-time, occupation, and threshold feeds;
- country heatmap activity totals;
- Opportunity Radar evidence and scores.

The Opportunity Radar remains stricter than the monitoring views: even when filters are
selected, only human-published regulatory changes can contribute.

## Operator workspace

`/global-intelligence` now provides six responsive filter controls, per-option evidence
counts, matched-versus-available totals, and a clear-all action. Every change card shows
its freshness, coverage posture, authority, confidence, materiality, and review state.

## Database and safety boundary

No schema change is required. Migration head remains
`0026_document_access_grants`.

The filter layer does not mutate sources, monitors, changes, verified rules, registry
assessments, pathways, profiles, comparisons, timelines, or client conclusions.
