## 1. Accepted behavior

H.2.1 adds one deterministic, aggregate-scoped recurrence guard to the accepted H.1 Eligibility Immune Circuit:

```text
verifier disagreement #1 -> WARNING / circuit CLOSED
verifier disagreement #2 -> WARNING / circuit CLOSED
verifier disagreement #3 -> WARNING + recurrence threshold reached
                           -> circuit OPEN atomically
fresh execution afterward -> blocked before provider egress
```

The incident itself remains WARNING. The repeated pattern, not a single disagreement, causes the restrictive transition.

The policy applies only to `verifier_disagreement` in this increment. Runtime-health, revision-conflict and reassessment-rollback warnings do not contribute to the threshold.

Authorized human-admin circuit close starts a fresh recurrence epoch. Recovery restores only the ability to attempt execution and grants no authority or autonomy.

## 2. Concurrency and transaction acceptance

The accepted implementation evaluates recurrence and the current circuit state inside the existing aggregate Activity-stream serialization boundary.

The PostgreSQL-only concurrency contract proves that when two sessions concurrently add threshold-crossing disagreement warnings, both warning incidents may become durable but exactly one circuit OPEN transition is appended.

This closes the stale-CLOSED race identified during implementation review.
