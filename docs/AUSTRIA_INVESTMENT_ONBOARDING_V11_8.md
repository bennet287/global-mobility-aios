## Narrow evidence scope

The pending proposal records:

- The applicant is a third-country national proposing self-employed activity in Austria.
- The core test is macroeconomic benefit beyond the undertaking's own operational benefit.
- A sustained transfer of at least EUR 100,000 is one official indicator. Job creation or preservation, know-how or new technology, and regional significance are also listed.
- The Public Employment Service evaluates macroeconomic benefit, while the residence authority separately decides the residence application.
- If issued, the Red-White-Red Card is valid for 24 months and covers the self-employed activity described in the application.

The EUR 100,000 figure is not treated as automatic eligibility. The draft explicitly preserves authority review, general requirements, case-specific evidence, capital risk, and change risk.
