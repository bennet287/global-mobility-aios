## 1. Acceptance decision

V1.3-H.2.4 is accepted and sealed.

The increment makes one bounded optimistic-concurrency race durable and explainable: a reassessment starts against the exact current canonical eligibility revision, the producer executes once, another governed operation advances the same canonical aggregate during producer latency, and the immediate post-producer revalidation detects that advancement before verifier egress or canonical effect.

The increment is observation/provenance only. It does not treat ordinary contention as an automatic safety fault and does not grant authority, autonomy or permission.

Permanent boundaries:

> The Immune System may restrict or stop execution. It never grants authority, autonomy or permission.

> Optimistic-concurrency contention is not automatically a safety fault.

## 2. Accepted classification boundary

H.2.4 attribution is accepted only when:

```text
canonical eligibility aggregate exists
exactly one ACTIVE revision vN exists
caller explicitly expects vN
E.2 accepts that exact precondition before producer egress
producer provider returns successfully
same canonical aggregate advances to a newer ACTIVE revision vM, M > N
immediate post-producer revision revalidation detects the advancement
verifier provider has not been called
no canonical effect from the stale attempt has been committed
```

The accepted low-level state signal is `EligibilityRevisionPostResolutionAdvance`.

That subtype proves canonical state advancement only. It does not itself claim provider egress. Provider-egress meaning is applied only at the trusted E.2/G.4 post-producer boundary.

The following remain excluded:

```text
H.2.3 pre-egress stale reassessment conflict
missing or future revision expectations
concurrent first canonical creation
multiple-ACTIVE-revision corruption
missing/deleted canonical lineage
producer runtime/provider failure
malformed producer output
Profile/Evidence/policy/context drift
verifier-stage revision races
G.2/G.3 revision races
database transaction rollback
generic request/schema errors
```

## 5. Integrity, replay and historical-truth acceptance

Acceptance proves:

- attribution + warning are committed as one transactional pair;
- an injected failure after attribution staging rolls the pair back;
- exact replay reuses the existing pair rather than duplicating it;
- a torn pair fails closed;
- replay with changed trusted producer runtime identity fails closed;
- replay with a changed revision snapshot under the same incident key fails closed;
- a historical `v1 -> v2` attribution remains replayable after a legitimate `v3` later supersedes `v2`;
- `observed_current_lifecycle_status = active` is treated as an event-time fact rather than a requirement that the historical revision remain current forever;
- concurrent first-time creation does not create H.2.4 attribution;
- H.2.3 pre-egress conflict remains classified as H.2.3 rather than being broadened into H.2.4;
- the stale attempt calls the producer exactly once and the verifier zero times;
- the stale attempt commits no new canonical effect;
- repeated H.2.4 warnings do not open the aggregate circuit.
